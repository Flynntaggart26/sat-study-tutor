# Geometry & Trigonometry (~15%)

Lines, angles, triangles, circles, area/volume, right-triangle trig, unit circle basics.

## High-yield
- Special triangles: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 45-45-90 (x,x,x√2), 30-60-90 (x,x√3,2x).
- Circle: `(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2`, circumference 2πr, area πr². Arc = fraction × circumference.
- Volume: prism lwh, cylinder πr²h, sphere 4/3πr³, cone 1/3πr²h (formulas given on test).
- Trig: SOHCAHTOA. sin=opp/hyp, cos=adj/hyp, tan=opp/adj.
- Draw every time. Label lengths/angles. Plug numbers for variables.

## Drill
1 geometry set/week. Redraw + Desmos geometry check. Memorize triples + circle equation.
