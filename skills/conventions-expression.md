# Conventions + Expression of Ideas (v2) — Cheapest RW Points

~40% of RW, fastest to fix to 700 RW. Master in this order (1 rule/week):

## 1. Boundaries / Punctuation (50% of conventions)

- Two independents CANNOT join with comma (splice). Fix: `.` `;` `:` + FANBOYS `—` or make dependent.
- Colon `:` = list/explanation after full sentence. Dash `—` = emphasis/interruption. Semicolon `;` = equal link.
- Apostrophe: `it's` = it is, `its` = possessive. `their/there/they're`, `your/you're` — test loves these.
- Shortest correct wins: if two choices both grammatical, pick concise (no redundancy).

Example: "The data was clear ___ the theory held." → needs `;` / `.` / `, and` — not `,`.

## 2. Verbs / Pronouns / Modifiers

- Subject-verb: ignore prepositional fluff ("The group of students *is*...").
- Tense: timeline consistent. Past story → past. General truth → present.
- Pronoun must have clear singular/plural antecedent. Dangling modifier must touch what it modifies.

## 3. Transitions + Notes Questions

Read sentence BEFORE + AFTER blank. Ask: contrast? cause? example? continuation?
- Contrast: however, whereas, conversely, despite
- Cause/result: thus, consequently, therefore
- Example: for instance, such as
- Emphasis/continuation: moreover, indeed, in fact

Notes → sentence Qs: use relevant info only, no extra claim, match question goal ("emphasize similarity" vs "present claim").

## Drill

Erica Meltzer 1 ch/day + 11 Q timed set same tag. Log rule verbatim + rewrite sentence correctly. Target 90% Easy/Medium before Hard.
