# Conventions + Expression of Ideas (cheapest RW points)

## Standard English Conventions (~13-15 Q)
Order to master: 1) punctuation (commas, colons, dashes, apostrophes) 2) verbs (tense/agreement) 3) pronouns/modifiers 4) parallelism.

Rules:
- Independent clauses join with . ; : + FANBOYS or dash. No comma splice.
- Verb must agree in number, tense fits timeline.
- Keep it short: shortest grammatically correct choice often wins (concision).

## Expression of Ideas (~12-14 Q)
Transitions: contrast (however, whereas), cause (thus, consequently), example (for instance). Read sentence before + after blank.
Notes → sentence: pick choice that uses relevant info only, no extra fluff.

## Drill
Erica Meltzer 1 chapter/day + 11-Q timed set. Log rule missed, rewrite sentence correctly.
