# Reading & Writing — Info & Ideas + Craft & Structure

Digital RW: 54 Q total, short passages (25-150 words), 1 Q each. Types: main idea, evidence, inference, vocab-in-context, cross-text connections, structure/purpose.

## Method
1. Read passage briefly, read Q stem, predict answer before choices.
2. Evidence Qs: find lines that prove answer, eliminate 3 that don't match.
3. Vocab-in-context: plug each choice into sentence, pick logical + tone match.
4. Cross-text: summarize Text1 vs Text2 relation (agree/disagree/extend) first.
5. Time: 1.1 min/Q. If >90 sec, guess + flag + move.

## Drill (3x/week, 30 min)
- 11-Q timed set from Question Bank (1 tag only) → review paraphrase for each miss.
- Vocab: 10 roots/day (Anki) from misses only (e.g. mitigate, underscore, contend).

## Killers
- Outside knowledge (use passage only), extreme words (always/never), half-right choices.
