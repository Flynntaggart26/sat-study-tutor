# Reading & Writing — Info & Ideas + Craft & Structure (v2)

Digital RW: 54 Q, 25-150 word passages, 1 Q each. ~60% of RW. Types + share:
- Central idea / main purpose (~15%) — read first+last sentence, match scope (not too narrow/broad).
- Evidence / inference (~20%) — find 1-2 lines that prove it. 3 choices lack support.
- Vocab-in-context (~12%) — plug each choice in, tone + logic must fit.
- Cross-text connections (~10%) — Text1 vs Text2: agree / disagree / extend? Summarize relation first.
- Structure / purpose (~8%) — why sentence/paragraph exists (introduce, concede, counterexample).

## 5-Step Method (every Q)

1. Glance Q stem first (evidence? vocab? purpose?).
2. Read passage once, 20-30 sec, underline shift words (but/however/yet).
3. Predict in 5 words before looking at choices.
4. Eliminate 3: outside knowledge, extreme (always/never), half-right (one word off).
5. Pick + mark confidence. >90 sec → guess, flag, move (1.1 min/Q).

## Worked Mini-Example

Q: "Which choice best states main idea?" → Read → predict "Bees decline due to habitat, not pesticides alone" → eliminate: (A) too narrow (pesticides only), (B) outside (economy — not in text), (C) matches, (D) extreme (all bees extinct). Pick C.

## Drills (3x/week)

- Mon: 11 Q Information & Ideas timed 14 min (QBank Medium) → log trap type per miss.
- Wed: 11 Q Craft/Structure + Vocab → make Anki: Front "underscore (v) in science passage" → Back "emphasize + your sentence".
- Fri: Mixed 11 Q Hard → review only flagged.

## Killers

- Using background knowledge. SAT passage is the world.
- Picking true-but-not-asked (supports detail, not main idea).
- Spending 3 min on 1 hard Q in easy Module 1 (caps hard Module 2).

Target: 85%+ Medium before Hard. 200 roots > 1000 words (see AI prompt #5).
