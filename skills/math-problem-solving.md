# Math — Problem Solving & Data (v2, ~15%)

Ratios, percents, units, scatter, mean/median, margin, probability. 50% misses here are reading, not math.

## Tactics with Examples

- **Percent:** change = (new-old)/old ×100. “% of” = ×decimal. “Increase to 120%” ≠ “increase by 120%”.
- **Ratio/proportion:** write `a/b = c/d`, cross-multiply, keep units (`miles/hour`).
- **Units:** write every step. Convert before calc (min→hours, cm→m). Circle answer unit to verify.
- **Stats:** median resists outliers, mean shifts. Larger sample → smaller margin. Margin = “likely true value ±m”.
- **Scatter:** describe trend + outlier, estimate line roughly — don’t overfit points.
- **Probability:** wanted/total. “Given” shrinks denominator.

## Anti-Traps

Estimate choices first (eliminate 2 absurd), compute to decide last 2. Never 5-min brute force — Desmos/table or guess+flag.

## Drill

Mixed 10 Q / 18 min weekly. Review: highlight where question states ask + unit. Rewrite misread Q correctly by hand.
