# Math — Problem Solving & Data Analysis (~15%)

Ratios, percents, units, scatterplots, mean/median/mode, margin of error, probability.

## Tactics
- Percent change = (new-old)/old. “% of” = multiply decimal.
- Units: write units every step, convert before calculating.
- Stats: median = middle (resists outliers), mean shifts. Margin = range of likely true value.
- Scatter: trend + outlier, don't overfit. Estimate line roughly.
- Probability = wanted/total. Read “given” condition carefully.

## Avoid
Over-calculating. Estimate choices first, compute only to eliminate 2.

## Drill
Mixed 10-Q timed (18 min). Review misreads — 50% of misses here are reading, not math.
