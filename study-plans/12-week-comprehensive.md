# 12-Week Comprehensive 900→1300+ (1-2h/day)

Kaplan/Princeton + Khan basics. Pre-algebra → algebra, sentence structure. No mocks. Flashcards daily.

## Phase 2 W5-8 Technique
College Panda Math half + Meltzer Grammar. 1 set/day timed. Mock 1 W8. Goal 1150+.

## Phase 3 W9-12 Performance
Bluebook 3-6, 1/week. Hard sets, Desmos, mixed review. W11-12 simulation + taper.
Full-time (4h/day) compress to 8 weeks. <1h/day extend Phase 1 to 6 weeks.
