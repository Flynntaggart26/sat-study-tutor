# Tutor Framework (Human Tutors)

## Diagnostic (60 min)
15 interview (goal, date, past PSAT/Bluebook) + 20 mini-set (11 RW + 11 Math timed) + 15 triage top 3 blockers + 10 plan + homework.

## Lessons
**60-min Math:** 10 review homework (student explains) + 20 timed set + 25 live solve (think-aloud, max 3 patterns) + 5 assign redo.
**60-min RW:** 10 vocab/conventions check + 20 timed passage set + 25 evidence review + 5 assign.
**90-min Mock review:** student pre-logged errors, tutor validates strategy, focus on timing/careless.

## Rules
Max 3 patterns/session. Correct → why → student re-solves → new Q. End 1 win + 2 actions.
Homework: 3 timed sets/week + redo misses. No redo = no new set.
Every 4 lessons: timed re-test, update tracker.
