# Books Roadmap by Score

## 900-1100 → Foundations
1. `Kaplan or Princeton Review Total Prep` — format + easy drills
2. `College Panda Math (first half)` + Khan conventions
Goal: algebra basics, sentence structure, 1100 timed.

## 1100-1300 → Technique
1. `College Panda Math (full)` — 1 chapter/day + drill
2. `Erica Meltzer Grammar` — punctuation → verbs → transitions
3. Bluebook 1-2 untimed → timed
Goal: medium modules solid, 1300.

## 1300-1500 → Precision
1. `Erica Meltzer Reading` + UWorld hard sets
2. `College Panda 10 tests` + 1600.io videos for misses
3. Desmos fluency (systems, quadratics graphed)
Goal: hard module, ≤8 misses/section.

## 1500+ → Polish
- Bluebook 5-6 only + Question Bank hard tagged Qs
- Error log only, no new books. 2 mocks/week review.

Libraries have Princeton/Kaplan. Buy: 1 Math (Panda) + 1 RW (Meltzer).
